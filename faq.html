Frequently asked questions

Can I use the image tools now?

Local image processing and export are available in the studio preview. Some operations depend on browser support and device memory.

Can I purchase a plan?

Not yet. Advance, Pro, Professional and Premium are proposed plans. No payment is collected or activated.

Does the SEO tool use AI?

No. It generates an editable local draft from your subject and image metadata. AI recognition is not connected.

Are my images uploaded?

The local editing tools use your browser canvas. Separate account services may communicate with Firebase.
