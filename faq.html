Frequently asked questions

Are the design tools free?

Yes. You can use all twelve tools without signing in.

Which formats can I export?

SVG and PNG are available. CSS tools also generate CSS code, and contrast and brand tools provide text results.

Where are favorites stored?

Favorites are stored locally in your browser. They are not synchronized to your account.

What happened to the old PDF and image tools?

The old toolkit has been retired and replaced by a new design workspace.
