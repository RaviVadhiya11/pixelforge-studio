Frequently Asked Questions

Practical answers about using PixelFire Studio's browser-based image and PDF utilities.

Are the image and PDF tools free?

The tools currently available on PixelFire Studio are offered free to use. Some features rely on third-party libraries or model downloads, and normal internet or device costs may apply.

Are my selected files uploaded to PixelFire Studio?

Core image and PDF processing is designed to happen in your browser. The website does not intentionally send selected files to a PixelFire-owned processing server. Your browser may contact third-party providers to download scripts, fonts, libraries or model files. Read our Privacy Policy for details.

Does image upscaling use AI?

The current resize and enlargement tools use browser-based resampling. They can increase pixel dimensions, but they do not reconstruct missing image detail or provide AI super-resolution.

Why can background removal take time or fail?

Background removal may need to download a machine-learning model the first time it runs. It can be slow or fail on a weak connection, unsupported browser, low-memory phone or very large image. Try a smaller image, close other browser tabs and retry.

Which image formats are supported?

Supported input formats depend on the browser's image decoder. JPG, PNG and WebP are commonly supported; other formats such as HEIC, TIFF, SVG, BMP or AVIF may vary by browser. Output options also depend on browser encoder support.

Why did my converted image become larger?

Some formats and quality settings produce larger files than the original. Compare the output size and try another format or quality setting. Compression does not guarantee a smaller file in every case.

Can I use the PDF tools for large documents?

PDF processing uses browser memory and can be demanding on mobile devices. Large documents or files with many pages may take longer or fail. Split a large task into smaller files where possible and keep the original document.

Do I need an account?

An account is not required for the core file tools. Optional sign-in features may be available for account-related functions.
