Frequently asked questions

Does upscaling restore lost detail?

Simple resampling cannot fully recover details that are not in the original.

Can I upscale on mobile?

Smaller images are recommended because large outputs use phone memory.

Which formats are supported?

Browser image decoding and selected output format determine support.

PixelForge Studio tools run in your browser where possible. File-format support, performance and output quality depend on your browser and device. Keep a copy of important original files.
