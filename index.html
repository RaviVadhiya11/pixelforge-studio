Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you get started.

Are the tools free?

The current browser-based image workspace is free to use. Hosting, domain, traffic and any future premium services may have separate costs.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. The current image processing runs locally in your browser. Your files remain on your device unless you choose to share them elsewhere.

Does 8K mean AI upscaling?

No. 8K changes pixel dimensions using browser resampling. It cannot reliably restore details that are not present in the original image.

Why did AVIF export fail?

AVIF encoding is not available in every browser. Choose JPG, PNG or WebP if your device does not support AVIF output.
