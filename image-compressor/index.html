How to use this free online tool

  1. Open the tool using the button above.

  4. Review the output and download it if it meets your needs.

Frequently asked questions

Will compression always reduce size?

No. Some files may not become smaller, depending on source format and settings.

Can I choose quality?

Yes, the compressor provides a quality control for supported formats.

Are my images uploaded?

Current image processing is performed in your browser.

PixelForge Studio tools run in your browser where possible. File-format support, performance and output quality depend on your browser and device. Keep a copy of important original files.
