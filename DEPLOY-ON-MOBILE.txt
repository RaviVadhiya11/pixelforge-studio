PIXELFORGE STUDIO — MOBILE GUIDE

Repository: https://github.com/RaviVadhiya11/pixelforge-studio
Site URL after Pages is enabled: https://RaviVadhiya11.github.io/pixelforge-studio/

1. Open repository Settings.
2. Select Pages.
3. Under Build and deployment choose Deploy from a branch.
4. Branch: main; folder: /(root); press Save.
5. Wait for the Pages deployment to finish, then open the site URL.

The repository already contains index.html and website policy/support files.
GitHub Pages is free static hosting. It does not configure payments, login, a database,
server-side AI, or ad revenue. Check every tool on your phone after deployment.
Personalize the contact email in privacy.html and terms.html before publishing.
