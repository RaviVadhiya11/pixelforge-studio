DocNexa Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PixelFire Studio ("we", "us") handles information when you use the DocNexa - PDF Scanner & Editor Android application ("DocNexa" or the "App"). DocNexa is offered free of charge and may display advertisements.

Information we handle

  • Account information: If you register or sign in, our authentication provider, Supabase, processes your email address, account identifier and authentication/session information to create and maintain your account and support sign-in, email confirmation and password recovery.
  • Documents and files: Documents you scan or select are processed on your device for the document features you choose. If you use cloud sync, the PDF files you choose to upload are sent to Supabase Storage and associated with your account so that you can list and download your synced files.
  • AI feature content: When you choose an AI feature, extracted text from the PDF and the task or prompt you submit are sent to our Supabase-hosted AI service for processing by Google Gemini. The AI service returns a response to the App. Do not submit highly sensitive or confidential information unless you have considered the risks and are authorized to do so.
  • Camera: Camera access is used when you choose to scan documents. Camera permission is not required for features that do not use the camera.
  • Notifications: If you allow notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging may use a device registration token to deliver app notifications.
  • Diagnostics and app usage: Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics and Performance may process app interaction events, diagnostic/crash information, device and app information, and performance data to understand usage, diagnose failures and improve reliability.
  • Advertising: Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) may process device identifiers, IP address, app interaction and advertising-related information to deliver, measure and help prevent invalid activity for ads. Advertising personalization and consent choices may depend on your location, device settings and applicable law.

How we use information

We use the information described above to provide scanning, PDF tools, account access, optional cloud sync and AI features; deliver notifications you allow; display and measure advertisements; maintain security; diagnose errors; and improve the App.

Service providers and sharing

DocNexa relies on third-party service providers, including Supabase for authentication, cloud storage and AI request handling; Google Gemini for AI responses; and Google Firebase and Google Mobile Ads for app analytics, diagnostics, performance, notifications and advertising. These providers process information as needed to provide their services under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not sell your personal information.

Storage, security and retention

Cloud-synced PDFs are stored in Supabase Storage under your account. Account and authentication information is handled by Supabase. AI requests are processed by our Supabase-hosted service and Google Gemini. Diagnostic, analytics, notification and advertising information may be retained by the relevant providers according to their settings and retention policies. We use service providers and reasonable safeguards, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Documents that you process only on your device remain under your device's control unless you choose a feature that sends them to a cloud or AI service. Keep your device and app updated and avoid sharing sensitive documents unnecessarily.

Your choices and permissions

You can choose whether to grant camera and notification permissions through Android settings. You can choose whether to use account-based cloud sync and AI features. You can control or reset advertising identifiers and ad personalization through your Android and Google settings, where available. Denying optional permissions may limit the related feature but should not prevent unrelated app features from working.

Account and data deletion requests

To request deletion of your DocNexa account and associated cloud data, contact us from your registered email address at business@pixelfirestudio.in with the subject "DocNexa Account Deletion". We will verify the request and process deletion, except for information we are required or permitted to retain for legal, security or legitimate operational purposes. Some information held by third-party providers may be subject to their own retention policies.

Important: This contact method is a deletion request channel. An in-app account deletion control and a dedicated web account-deletion request page must also be made available before the App is submitted to Google Play if account creation is supported.

Children's privacy

DocNexa is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App, its features or service providers change. The latest revision date will be shown at the top of this page.
