Payments, cancellations and refunds

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This page explains the current status of purchases for PixelFire Studio and its software products. Policies for an active purchase will also depend on the terms shown at checkout and the applicable payment provider or app store.

Current purchasing availability

The existing PixelFire Studio image and PDF tools are free to use. ToolNexa AI Pro is displayed as a planned ₹199/month subscription; its sandbox integration does not create a live customer purchase or activate premium access. DocNexa app-store and paid-subscription availability must be confirmed before any purchase.

Before a paid subscription launches

A live checkout must clearly disclose the billed amount and currency, billing frequency, taxes where applicable, what is included, when the subscription renews, how customers can cancel, and the applicable refund terms. Until these details are published at the relevant product's checkout, no live subscription should be sold through this site.

Cancellation and refund requests

If you have already paid for a product through an authorized payment provider or app store, please use that provider's subscription-management or refund flow when available. You may also contact us with the product name, transaction date, and provider transaction reference to request assistance. Do not email card numbers, passwords or other payment credentials. Refund eligibility depends on the applicable checkout terms, provider policies and consumer law; this informational page does not override statutory rights.
