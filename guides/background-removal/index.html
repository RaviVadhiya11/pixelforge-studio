Remove simple image backgrounds and preserve transparency

Understand color-based background removal and edge cleanup.

Know the method

The remover uses color and edge-related settings, not a neural subject-segmentation service. Consistent backgrounds with good contrast from the subject usually work best. Hair, glass, reflections and similar foreground/background colors can be difficult.

Adjust gradually

Load an image and try automatic or color-picking controls where appropriate. Increase tolerance in small steps; excessive tolerance may erase parts of the foreground. Feathering can soften a hard edge but can also introduce halos. Compare the output against the source and return to the original if necessary.

Export and inspect

Use PNG for transparent results; JPEG has no alpha channel. Examine the saved image on light and dark backgrounds to reveal remaining color fringes. Complex subjects may need manual masking or specialist segmentation. Automated background removal is not a guarantee of official ID-photo compliance.
