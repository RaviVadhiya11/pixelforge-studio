Prepare photos for sharing: metadata and private details

Review both the picture and the information stored inside the file.

Two kinds of information

Photos can reveal names, addresses, documents and vehicle plates in their visible pixels. Image metadata can contain dates, camera details or location. Removing embedded metadata does not remove information visible in the photograph.

Create a separate export

EXIF Metadata Cleaner re-encodes image pixels in the browser. Such re-encoding normally does not copy source EXIF information, but check with an independent metadata reader when privacy matters. Keep the original privately and share the verified copy.

Blur is not guaranteed redaction

Blur and pixelation can obscure detail for creative use, but do not guarantee irreversible redaction. For sensitive text or identifiers, use a suitable editor to cover the entire region with an opaque block, flatten the image and inspect the actual saved copy. Check reflections and background screens too.
