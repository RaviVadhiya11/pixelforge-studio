Improve photo tone, sharpness and resolution

Use restrained adjustments and understand the limits of enlargement.

Start with tone

Use Color & Light Studio to adjust brightness and contrast before sharpening. Shadow Recovery raises darker tones, Highlight Softening reduces bright tones, and Auto Levels stretches tonal range. A strong correction can remove an intentionally low-key mood. Clipped highlights cannot be reconstructed merely by darkening them.

Balance noise and detail

Detail Sharpen increases local edge contrast and may also exaggerate grain. Noise Smoothing reduces fine fluctuations but can soften texture. Inspect the result at the intended display size and keep a clean source copy for comparison.

Enlarge responsibly

Enhancer & 4X Upscaler uses browser resampling and sharpening, not neural super-resolution. Output dimensions depend on settings and device limits. Enlarging cannot guarantee reconstruction of unreadable text or missing facial detail. Verify the final exported file before printing or publishing.
