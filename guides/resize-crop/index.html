Resize, crop and fit photos for social media

Prepare images for different aspect ratios without stretching subjects.

Aspect ratio and dimensions

A square is 1:1, while a 1280 by 720 thumbnail is 16:9. Proportional resizing keeps the shape of the source. Cropping removes content to fill a frame. Fitting a photo inside a canvas preserves the entire image but may add margins.

Choose a workflow

Use Precision Resizer for exact dimensions with aspect ratio locked. Use Interactive Cropper to frame the subject. Thumbnail Fit Studio produces a 16:9 layout with adjustable padding; Canvas Padding adds a border to artwork. Compare both crop and fit when edges contain important details.

Example

For a landscape product photograph, make a square crop if the surrounding background is unimportant. For a wide thumbnail that must retain the entire product, use the fit tool with a suitable background. Keep text and faces away from edges, inspect dimensions in the output bar, and check the destination platform's current requirements.
