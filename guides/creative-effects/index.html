Create photo effects with duotone, grain and gradients

Combine effects to develop a consistent visual style.

Choose a palette

Duotone Mixer maps luminance to two colors. Gradient Overlay adds a gradual color layer. Select colors that distinguish the subject clearly and keep possible text overlays readable.

Build a sequence

Correct tone first, then apply Duotone Mixer. Use the chain option when available to make the edited result the source for another tool. Add Film Grain sparingly. Keep a separate clean image to compare creative directions.

Match the output

Halftone Print creates dot patterns, Posterize reduces tonal levels and Chromatic Aberration offsets color channels. These are stylization effects rather than restoration. Fine patterns may vanish or become distracting at small sizes. Review the downloaded result and avoid repeatedly compressing the same version.
