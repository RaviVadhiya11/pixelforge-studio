Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy describes the current PixelFire Studio website and its browser-based image and PDF utilities. Please read it before creating an account or using a tool.

Files selected for tools

Image and PDF files selected for the tools are processed in your browser where the feature and browser allow it. PixelFire Studio does not intentionally upload those selected files to a PixelFire-owned file-processing server. Some features download scripts, libraries or AI model files from third-party content delivery networks. Your browser may send those providers technical information such as your IP address and browser request details. Background removal and PDF tools may need substantial memory and an internet connection to fetch their libraries or models.

Accounts and authentication

If you choose to create an account or sign in, the website uses Firebase Authentication provided by Google Firebase. The authentication provider processes information such as your email address, authentication credentials and, if supplied, display name to create and manage your account, verify your email and support sign-in or password reset. PixelFire Studio does not receive or store your password in its own website database. Do not use an account password that you use on another service. You can use the file tools without signing in; an account is not required for the core tools.

For information about Firebase data handling, review the Firebase privacy and security information and Google's privacy policy.

Hosting, cookies and advertising

The website is hosted using GitHub Pages. At present, PixelFire Studio has not installed Google AdSense advertising code or its own analytics code, and Google ads are not currently served on this website. If Google AdSense is enabled in the future, Google and its partners may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar identifiers to collect information as a result of ad serving, including to provide, measure and personalize advertising where permitted by law and user settings. Third-party vendors, including Google, may place and read cookies on your browser or use web beacons or IP addresses when serving ads on this website. You can learn how Google uses information from sites and apps that use its services at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use Google services. You can manage ad personalization through Google My Ad Center. Before any advertising or analytics service is activated, this policy and any legally required consent notices will be reviewed and updated to reflect the services actually in use.

Third-party libraries and services

The site may load fonts, JavaScript libraries, PDF processing libraries and AI model assets from third-party providers including Google Fonts, jsDelivr and cdnjs. Those providers may receive technical request information when your browser contacts them. Their own privacy policies apply to their services.

Data security and retention

Use a secure, up-to-date browser and avoid processing files on a shared device. PixelFire Studio does not operate a separate file storage account for tool uploads. Account information is handled by Firebase Authentication according to its service configuration and retention practices. For account deletion or authentication questions, contact us using the address below.

Children

The tools are general-purpose utilities and are not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate permission.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when tools, hosting, authentication, advertising or analytics change. The latest revision date appears at the top of this page.
