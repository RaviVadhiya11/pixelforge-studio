Request DocNexa Account Deletion

App: DocNexa - PDF Scanner & Editor
Developer: PixelFire Studio
Last updated: 17 November 2026

You can request deletion of your DocNexa account and the personal data associated with it, including cloud-synced documents stored in your DocNexa account.

How to submit a deletion request

  1. Send an email from the email address registered with your DocNexa account.
  2. Use the subject line DocNexa Account Deletion Request.
  3. In the message, include the registered email address and state that you want your DocNexa account and associated data deleted. Do not send your password or verification codes.
  4. We may contact you to verify that you own the account before processing the request.

Email a deletion request

Or email business@pixelfirestudio.in.

What will be deleted

  • Your DocNexa account, after ownership verification and completion of the deletion process.
  • PDF files and other documents stored in DocNexa cloud storage and associated with your account, where controlled by us.
  • Other account-linked information that we control and no longer need to retain.

What may be retained

We may retain limited information where required or permitted by law, for security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution or legitimate record-keeping. Backups and records held by service providers may take additional time to expire under their retention processes. Information processed independently by third-party providers may be subject to those providers' own policies.

Processing time and status

We will review and verify your request, then process it within the period required by applicable law. If we need more information or cannot complete the request, we will contact you using the email address provided. Sending an email submits a request; it does not mean deletion has already occurred.

Before you request deletion: Download any cloud-synced documents you want to keep. Deletion of your account and cloud files may be permanent.

Privacy information

Read the DocNexa Privacy Policy for details about information handling and service providers.
