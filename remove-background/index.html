Frequently asked questions

Does it upload my photo?

The current workflow processes the selected file in your browser; external model files may be downloaded.

Can I download a transparent PNG?

The tool is designed to export a PNG with transparency when processing succeeds.

What images work best?

Photos with a clear subject and visible edges usually give better results.
